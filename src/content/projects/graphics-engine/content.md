## Overview

This project is a graphics engine built from the ground up in **C++ and DirectX**, developed during my Games Engineering master's at the University of Warwick. The goal was to understand every stage of the pipeline by implementing it myself: rasterization, rendering, ray tracing and path tracing.

## What I built

- A software rasterizer with perspective-correct interpolation
- A real-time renderer on DirectX
- A ray tracer and a path tracer with physically-based materials
- 2D and 3D demo projects running on the engine

![Engine screenshot placeholder](./cover.svg)

## What I learned

Writing an engine end to end makes every abstraction in commercial engines click: why scene graphs look the way they do, what the GPU driver is actually doing, and where the performance goes.
